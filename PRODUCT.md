# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Plant disease classification researchers managing reproducible local and notebook-based experiments.

## Product Purpose
Configure baseline/teacher/KD training, monitor execution and compare measured results for academic work.

## Primary Tasks
Create an experiment; find and follow a job; compare results within a dataset; export a portable notebook; recover or safely remove an experiment.

## Constraints
Native Streamlit, local-first, explicit long-training and deletion confirmation. Preserve immutable config snapshots, raw research records and existing model/data protocols.

## Product Language
Turkish actions and explanations; model identifiers, metric names and research terms retain their exact meaning.
